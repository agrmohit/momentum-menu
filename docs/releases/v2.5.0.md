Check for updates from the app, and see why Forget is off on new firmware. Changes since [v2.4.0](https://github.com/agrmohit/momentum-menu/releases/tag/v2.4.0).

## Updates

- **Checking for a new version:** choose **Check for Updates...** in the menu bar icon's right-click menu, or press **Check for Updates** in Settings > About. The app asks GitHub for the latest release and says whether a newer one exists, with a button to open its page. Before, the only way to know was to visit the repository.
- **When the app goes online:** only when you press Check for Updates. It never checks on its own, sends no account or device details, and downloads nothing.

## Paired devices

- **After the headphones update their firmware:** a line under the device list says Forget and Disconnect for other devices are not available until that firmware is tested. Before, they were greyed out with no reason given.

## Menu

- **Refresh and Disconnect while the headphones are not connected:** they are greyed out in the right-click menu. Before, they could still be chosen, and Disconnect then stopped the app from reconnecting to the headphones on its own.

## Install

Needs macOS 26 on Apple silicon.

1. Download the DMG, open it and drag Momentum Menu to Applications.
2. Open Momentum Menu. macOS shows **"MomentumMenu.app" Not Opened**. Press **Done**, not the blue button.
3. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and scroll down to **Security**. Next to **"MomentumMenu.app" was blocked to protect your Mac**, press **Open Anyway**, then confirm with Touch ID or your password.
4. macOS asks once more: **Open "MomentumMenu.app"?** Press **Open Anyway**. The blue button would move the app to the Bin.
5. Allow Bluetooth when asked.

Steps 2 to 4 are needed once, because the app is ad hoc signed, not notarised by Apple. Or clear the quarantine flag from a terminal:

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MomentumMenu.app
```

Optional: check the download was built by this repo's release workflow, from this tag, and belongs to this release, or compare its checksum with the `.sha256` file beside it:

```sh
gh attestation verify MomentumMenu-2.5.0.dmg -R agrmohit/momentum-menu
gh release verify-asset v2.5.0 MomentumMenu-2.5.0.dmg -R agrmohit/momentum-menu
shasum -a 256 -c MomentumMenu-2.5.0.dmg.sha256
```
