The first release of this fork. Everything since [andr3-santos/momentum-menu](https://github.com/andr3-santos/momentum-menu) v1.0.2.

## Popover

- Redesigned with native macOS 26 controls: a battery ring header over Control Center-style tiles. Needs macOS 26; the update checker is gone.
- Charging shows a breathing bolt in the ring and a Charging pill; Charged when full on the cable. The ring turns red at 20% and under.
- The playing codec and sample rate, from whichever device is streaming.
- Optional battery percentage in the menu bar.

## Noise control

- Off, Adaptive and Custom in one click each, with the level slider in Custom.
- Anti-wind: Off, Auto or Max.

## Sound

- Smart Control's eight equaliser presets, plus three of our own.
- Band sliders labelled 63, 250, 1k, 4k and 8k, as Smart Control shows them.
- Bass Boost.

## Headphone settings

- Touch controls, on-head detection, Smart Pause, auto-answer calls and Comfort Calls.

## Multipoint

- A line under the battery reads "Also on iPhone" or "Only this Mac". Click it for the paired devices.
- Connect a paired device while a slot is free.
- Disconnect one by hovering its green pill. For this Mac it is the usual hand-over; for another device it needs firmware 3.38.3 or newer, since older firmware was seen to erase a pairing instead.
- Settings picks a Phone, Tablet, Laptop or Desktop icon for each device.

## App

- Three app icons to pick in Settings: Starfield, Sleepy and Ghost.
- Cmd+W closes Settings, and Cmd+Q quits from Settings and the right-click menu.
- The bundle id is `com.agrmohit.momentummenu`. Coming from upstream, saved settings and Launch at Login start fresh.

## Safety and reliability

- Every command the app sends is on an allow-list with its exact payload; anything else is refused before it reaches the headphones.
- The model is checked before the first write, so other Sennheiser headphones are left alone.
- Writes are ordered, and a newer click or edit always wins over an older one still in flight.
- Fixed races between connecting, refreshing and changing settings, and between the equaliser and a refresh.
- The Bluetooth transport runs on the main actor.
- `build.sh` no longer installs a build that failed.

## Install

Needs macOS 26 on Apple silicon.

1. Optional: check the download was built by this repo's release workflow, from this tag. Either verify its attestation, or compare its checksum with the `.sha256` file beside it:

   ```sh
   gh attestation verify MomentumMenu-2.1.1.dmg -R agrmohit/momentum-menu
   shasum -a 256 -c MomentumMenu-2.1.1.dmg.sha256
   ```

2. Open the DMG and drag Momentum Menu to Applications.
3. The app is ad hoc signed, not notarised, so macOS blocks the first launch. The attestation does not change that: macOS checks only Apple signing. Open the app once, then choose **Open Anyway** in System Settings > Privacy & Security within the hour. Or clear the quarantine flag from a terminal:

   ```sh
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MomentumMenu.app
   ```

4. Open it and allow Bluetooth when asked.
